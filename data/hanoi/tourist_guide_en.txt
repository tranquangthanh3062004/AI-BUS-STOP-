=== HANOI PUBLIC TRANSPORT GUIDE FOR TOURISTS ===

1. BASIC BUS INFORMATION
- Ticket Price: A standard bus ride usually costs 8,000 VND to 10,000 VND depending on the distance.
- Payment: Pay in cash directly to the bus conductor. Please prepare small change (1,000 VND, 2,000 VND, or 5,000 VND notes) as conductors might not have enough change for large bills (500,000 VND).
- How to catch a bus: Wait at designated bus stops (marked with blue signs). When you see your bus approaching, wave your hand to signal the driver to stop. 
- Electric Buses (VinBus): Look for the green buses with an "E" prefix (e.g., E01, E02). They are modern, have air conditioning, free WiFi, and cost around 7,000 - 9,000 VND.

2. AIRPORT TRANSPORTATION (NOI BAI INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT - HAN)
- Route 86 (High-quality bus): Hanoi Railway Station - Hoan Kiem Lake - Noi Bai Airport. Price: 45,000 VND. Highly recommended for tourists with luggage.
- Route 68: Ha Dong - Noi Bai Airport. Price: 50,000 VND.
- Route 17: Long Bien - Noi Bai Airport. Price: 9,000 VND (Standard bus, slower).
- Route 07: Cau Giay - Noi Bai Airport. Price: 8,000 VND.

3. HANOI METRO (URBAN RAILWAY)
- Line 2A (Cat Linh - Ha Dong): Elevated railway connecting the city center (Cat Linh Station) to the southwestern part of Hanoi (Ha Dong). Ticket price: 8,000 - 15,000 VND per ride. A day pass is available for 30,000 VND (unlimited rides in a day).
- Line 3 (Nhon - Hanoi Station): The elevated section is currently operating. It connects the western suburbs to Cau Giay area.
- How to ride: Buy a plastic token at the ticket vending machine or counter, tap it at the gate to enter, and insert it into the slot when exiting.

4. BUS ROUTES TO MAJOR TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Hoan Kiem Lake & Old Quarter: Routes 08, 09, 14, 31, 36, 86. Note: During weekends (Friday night to Sunday night), buses will not enter the pedestrian zones around the lake.
- Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum: Routes 09, 22, 33, 45, 50.
- Temple of Literature (Van Mieu): Routes 02, 23, 32, 38, 41.
- West Lake (Ho Tay): Routes 09A, 13, 14, 33, 55, E09.
- Bat Trang Ceramic Village: Route 47A from Long Bien transit point.

5. LOST AND FOUND & EMERGENCY
- If you lose your belongings on a public bus, try to remember the bus number, license plate, and the time. Call the Transerco Hotline: 1900 1296 (Vietnamese speaker might be required).
- For safety, always keep an eye on your belongings, especially your phone and wallet, as pickpocketing can occur in crowded buses.
